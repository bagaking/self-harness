## Task

Produce one small, evidence-backed delivery that makes your background-goal / flash-suppression design operational.

Prefer a skill-first result:

- a new skill under `skills/` for branch agents that need to maintain a stable background goal, generate candidate flashes, suppress noisy candidates, and invest in one proof-bearing improvement;
